MAIN GAME STORY LINE

The main game story line is the story line when it starts when you join the game for the first time and ends when you beat the final boss.
To explain it simply, you got into accident, you escape the "game".

The Story

You are a person in real life who is named as Jake "Jill" Brown and lives in Thailand.
It was a normal day as a content creator/blogger/vlogger. Today, you are going to make a vlog showcasing your cool house.
And when you go to the road vlogging, you didn't get isekai-ed, (who would think of that wth) but you caught an eye on a poster on a brick wall opposite from your house.
It said "LAB rat(the rat was well hidden) ASSISTANTS NEEDED! GO TO -1/-5, MAI YAK JA AN ROAD, PAK NAM, SAMUTPRAKARN, SAMUTPRAKARN, THAILAND 10420"
You thought, why not go on a little side-hustle! And, you went to the lab.
You see nice scientists who are making a brand new invention: a teleporter to Roblox!
You got to sign in, but you were suprised that it was actually lab rat, not lab assistant!
You got in the teleporter, but, it malfunctioned! It instead teleports you to a place called The Gimmick World!
When you got to the gimmick world, TK immediately sees you. He greets you, shows you around, and tell how this world works!
You want to escape this world, so you go ask npcs to help you.
One of them, who is JPGuest, tells you about The Rope of The Sky Gimmick which a rope falls down and leads to the largest cloud.
You get that gimmick. Going up the rope, you meet TK again.
You both see a giant door that you both don't know what it is. Then, TK says that Bob might know about it.
You go and ask THGuest about Bob's whereabouts. And he tells you that Bob is on a mountain... which has an obby.
THGuest then gives you a master key, since there is a shed/house on the mountain, where Bob is in, and that specific shed/house is locked.
You climb the mountain, and see a shed/house on the mountain. You get the key and unlock the door.
You see Bob and go talk with him.
He said that the giant door needed a key, specifically The Amazing Key, which is only obtainable by a gimmick that is called The Amazing Key.
You tell TK and he says to you that you should wait for the gimmick. And he recommends you to stay on the clouds.
You get that gimmick and open the door. You go to the ???/the Void. Which is a void. yes.
You then encounter the final boss of the game, the creator(??? he sounds robotic???).
They said that they will execute you, but they want to see you go on these final challenges first.
There will be 5 stages in the final challenges. The first one is an obby.
TK comes along with you and realizes that he has voice-acting, which is unusual because in the gimmick world, he has... NNNNNN sfx. (typewriter revealing dialogue)
Then the second one is the logic part.
Third one: Rhythm. (its a rhythm game)
Fourth, Intellegence. All questions are randomized.
Lastly, stealth (all these stages are inspired by splatoon 2 octo expansion because escaping)
When you beat all the stage, you come to beat the "creator." TK evaporates (but doesn't die, instead, put aside)
Using the drums as a cue that the boss is vulnerable to damage, you win the battle!
Then the actual creator comes after getting his nice nice coffee and realizes that the robot creator gone insane
He teleports you back to real life! Yay! but he can't for TK because he's an NPC.
Happy ending!
