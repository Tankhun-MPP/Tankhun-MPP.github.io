INTRO

Score as a word basically a tally of what you've got, such as how much you've scored a goal, how much you ate hotdogs, and so on.
Score in this game is actually both a tally and not a tally of your performance.
Score is also the only currency in this game!
And, the score can be in the negatives. In this game, this is called being in debt.

Uses of Score

Score has not that many uses here:

  • Measure your performance: It measures the performance based on how long you lived, what gimmicks you have, and what you have done.
  • Buy in Otori's shop: Buy gears. and also random stuff like a stud block thingy and donation.
  • Compete in the best and worst scores: Leaderboard stuff. That's normal in games. More here.
  • Do specific things that costs score: like teleporting past an obby for 100.

For all purchases and costs in score, you can buy them even though if you don't have enough!
The only thing making you unable to purchase stuff is that you are in debt.

Getting Score

Getting score is very easy and has 2 ways of getting them. Both of them are going to be in seperate documents to be explained more in detail. But in here, it's going to be explained simply.

  1. Passive Score: You get (base) 1 score every second, where the gimmicks affect it and it will not happen when the gimmick timer pauses. (for afk-ing people)
  2. Active Score: For each (specific) action you do, such as jumping 5 times or climbing a mountain, you get a specific amount of score from it. It is like little achievements in a game where it gives you stuff from achieving them. (for active people)

Competitions between Score

In a game, there should be a competition in something such as speedrunning or getting the world record of something. In SGWH, the competitions also has speedrunning, and a thing called leaderboards.
Leaderboards in this game will rank between the best (most positive) score and the worst (most negative) score. For leaderboards, it doesn't rank with your latest score run, but actually your personal best/worst run! Your personal best starts at -inf, and personal worst starts at inf.
