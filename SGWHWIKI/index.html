Welcome to Some Gimmick Will Happen!

Some Gimmick Will Happen (SGWH) is a Roblox game made by Tankhun.
This is the official wiki for SGWH.

🎮GIMMICKS

    SGWH is a game where you get a gimmick at every 30 seconds.
Gimmicks can change everything in the game (locally if in the world).
An example of a gimmick is dwarf which makes you three times smaller than your current size then.
More on the gimmicks page.

📖STORY

    SGWH has lore and a story line that consists of a main line and a post-game line.
In the main line, it's a bit shorter since there's the gimmicks,
but for the post-game line, it will be a lot longer since the player actually wants the story and lore!
More on the story page!

🎲CHAOS

    i'm not making a hako baelz joke here.
In SGWH, the gimmicks make most of the chaos, and the players + items will make the remaining of the 100%!
