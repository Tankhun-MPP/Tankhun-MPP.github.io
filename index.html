GIMMICKS

Introduction

Gimmicks are an important mechanic/part of Some Gimmick Will Happen.
Gimmicks basically mean that there is something special in a thing, like when there's a gimmick in a game, that means that the game is special from the others in a way.
In this game, gimmick is the thing that can cause the chaos in the game that can change everything in the world (locally)

How to get a gimmick?

In this game, you can get a gimmick in every n seconds, where n starts at 30.
You're probably asking why is it n, but not 30. The reason is that gimmick can change n too.
If you are a normal player, this is the only way to get a gimmick.
But for a person who has admin powers, you can get them by running a command, or opening the Gimmick Panel then selecting a gimmick to toggle them.
Why is there a Gimmick Panel? Solely for testing.

How to remove a gimmick?

A gimmick can be removed by a NPC named Jinx
You talk to Jinx to ask to remove a gimmick.
Though, he will actually pick a random gimmick to remove, and if you don't have that specific gimmick to be removed,
You will be punished by turning your head upside down.
